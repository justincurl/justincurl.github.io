Justin Curl

Hello! I am a J.D. Candidate at Harvard Law School and am currently a Fellow at the MacArthur Foundation. Previously, I served as Technology Law & Policy Advisor to New Mexico Attorney General Raúl Torrez.

Before law school, I worked as an AI researcher at Microsoft Research Asia, was a Schwarzman Scholar at Tsinghua University in Beijing, and graduated magna cum laude with a degree in Computer Science from Princeton University, where I was fortunate to be advised by Arvind Narayanan.

I am interested in the intersection of technology and the law. You can find some of my writing below.
